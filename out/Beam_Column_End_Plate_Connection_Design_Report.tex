\section{Design Summary}

The beam-to-column end plate connection (WB 450 to HB 450, E 250 grade) was verified for Limit State Design under 15 kN axial, 210 kNm moment and 40 kN shear.

All checks pass except bolt tension including prying action (98.6 kN provided vs 105.0 kN required). Redesign by increasing bolt diameter to M24 or upgrading to Gr 9.8 is recommended.
